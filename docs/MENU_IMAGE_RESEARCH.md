# FAB FOODS — Menu image research

**Date:** 2026-11-17. **Status:** 101 optimized WebP assets prepared locally; no database writes.

## Purpose

Record one researched, freely licensed representative menu image candidate for each of the 111 products in `server/src/scripts/data/menu.pdf.manifest.js`. The machine-readable result is `server/src/scripts/data/menu-images.manifest.js`. The 101 sourced images are illustrative stock photography, not photographs of FAB FOODS dishes, and are downloaded as WebP assets under `client/public/images/menu/` by `npm.cmd run images:menu`; the manifest and assets have not been seeded into MongoDB or published.

## Research method

- Eight parallel research sessions covered the products in manifest order (indices 0–13, 14–27, 28–41, 42–55, 56–69, 70–83, 84–97 and 99–110). Their complete final JSON outputs were recovered verbatim (14 + 14 + 14 + 14 + 14 + 14 + 14 + 12 = 110 records), with no truncation or summaries.
- Index 98 (`strawberry-and-banana`) was absent from those outputs and was researched separately on 2026-11-17 using the Pexels search results page and the individual photo page.
- Each `sourceUrl`, `imageUrl`, photographer name and license was taken from the live Pexels page; no photographer, source, license or image URL was invented or inferred.
- Live web search returned transient HTTP 429 responses, so Pexels pages were fetched directly; all accepted records were verified against their source pages.
- Records were re-ordered to match the manifest exactly, with one record per slug and no unknown slugs. `filename` is `<slug>.webp` for every record and `localPath` is `/images/menu/<filename>` for sourced records.
